THE PROBLEM

A course can give you content.
A learning system gives you direction.

01

Where do I start?

A clear entry point based on the knowledge each skill requires.

02

Am I ready to move on?

Completion and mastery are measured separately, so progress means more.

03

What should I revise?

Weak areas become specific review actions instead of vague advice.

HOW IT WORKS

One path. Clear decisions.

Academy OS reads the relationship between topics, results, and practice to recommend the most useful action—not simply the next page.

  1. 01

    Understand the path

    See the skills, prerequisites, and outcomes before you begin.

  2. 02

    Learn and practise

    Use focused explanations, checks, and real support tasks.

  3. 03

    Prove understanding

    Assessment performance determines mastery and revision needs.

  4. 04

    Follow the next signal

    Continue, review, retry, or practise with a reason for every action.
